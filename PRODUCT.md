## Platform

web

## Users

1. **North American Retail Buyers & Category Managers**: Sourcing consumer-facing goods, requiring barcode compliance (GS1/UPC), customized retail packaging, strict defect tolerances (<0.5%), and synchronized seasonal deliveries.
2. **Wholesale & Commercial Distributors**: Ordering full container load (FCL) shipments, requiring factory floor pricing, institutional volume discounts, and uninterrupted logistics chain of custody.
3. **Institutional Trade Directors**: Seeking verified supplier governance, Western contract enforcement, and ESG/labor audit compliance in Asia manufacturing centers.

## Product Purpose

NAMAN INTERNATIONAL LTD provides considered, forensic international trade coordination and supply oversight between Asian manufacturing hubs (Guangdong, Zhejiang, Jiangsu) and North American ports. Success means zero unexpected customs delays, defect rates capped under agreed AQL thresholds, and predictable, accountable container transit.

## Positioning

Direct engineering supervision across the Pearl River Delta paired with Western institutional governance, trade finance coordination, and bonded free-port export clearance in Hong Kong.

## Operating Context

- **Manufacturing Centers**: Shenzhen, Dongguan, Ningbo, and Shanghai factory clusters.
- **Export Gateway**: Hong Kong free-port hub (customs clearance, consolidation, trade finance).
- **Destination Gateway**: North American ports (Long Beach, Los Angeles, Savannah, NY/NJ) to inland distribution centers.
- **Carrier Alliances**: FMC Licensed NVOCC, FIATA International, IATA Cargo Registered, with Maersk, Hapag-Lloyd, MSC, COSCO, and CMA CGM.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Factory Auditing & Sourcing**: Supplier vetting, financial health checks, factory capacity verification.
- **Quality Control**: AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspections, in-line QA, raw material verification.
- **Logistics & Customs**: FCL/LCL coordination, Incoterms governance (FOB, CIF, DDP), bill of lading administration.
- **Constraint**: Commercial trade programs operate under formal minimum order thresholds (MOQ) typical of container-level wholesale trade.
